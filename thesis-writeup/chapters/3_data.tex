\chapter{Data and bilateral remittance flow estimation}

Bilateral remittance flow data between US states and Mexican municipalities is not publicly available. We therefore estimate these flows ourselves, distributing state-level remittance outflows from the US to Mexican municipalities according to observed migration shares. 
The estimated inflows at the municipality level are then compared against official data to validate the accuracy of our procedure.

\section{Data sources}

Our main source of remittance data is the Banco de México (Banxico), which provides two complementary datasets through its Sistema de Información 
Económica (SIE). The first, series CE168, records remittance inflows from each US state to Mexico in millions of current US dollars at quarterly 
frequency, covering the period from Q1 2013 to Q1 2026 \parencite{banxico_ce168}. This series provides the US State outflows from which we derive our bilateral flow estimates. The second, series CE166, records remittance inflows received by each Mexican municipality, also in millions of current US dollars at quarterly frequency and over the same sample period \parencite{banxico_ce166}. This dataset serves as a benchmark to assess the accuracy of our estimates.
